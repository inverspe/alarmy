Night, then sunrise

Setup happens in a calm night indigo. When the alarm goes off, the whole screen turns sunlight yellow, like a wake-up light. While the last minutes ring out, a sun rises over the horizon until the alarm stops.

The clock and object names use SF Pro Expanded, very wide and heavy, so you can read them before your eyes are fully open.

On the iPhone the type is Apple's built-in SF Pro Expanded. This preview approximates it with Archivo at its widest setting. The sun animation here loops; in the app it rises once, in step with the countdown.
